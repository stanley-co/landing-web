## ADR-016: Approved MVP scope resolution (2026-07-18)

**Status:** Accepted. The fixed-scope decisions in [MVP_SCOPE_DECISIONS_2026-07-18.md](MVP_SCOPE_DECISIONS_2026-07-18.md) are binding. Use fixed models and DTOs for privacy policy, slides, leads, notifications, media and documents. `/about` and `/contacts` stay static. Do not implement page builder, legacy `/home`, certificates, contact/menu CRUD, lead retry, Telegram/Bitrix integrations or production migration in MVP.

## ADR-017: Entity identifier strategy (2026-07-18)

**Status:** Accepted. Each aggregate uses a backend-generated UUID v4 primary key. Create DTOs never accept `id`, `code` or `externalId`. `externalId` is nullable, unique when non-null and writable only by the deferred migrator/internal import; `code` is immutable and generated by backend (`PRD`, `CAT`, `MAT`, `SLD`, `MED`, `DOC`, `LED`). Public lookups resolve UUID first and fall back to `externalId`, preserving imported links without forcing slugs.

## ADR-018: Embedded media attachment workflow (2026-07-18)

**Status:** Accepted. A common Media Picker is used by product, content, slide, document and privacy forms. It has library and upload tabs, server-side search/filtering and single/multiple selection. A new editor creates an upload session, not an empty aggregate. Its uploaded files are `TEMPORARY`; saving the owner attaches eligible files and activates them transactionally. A scheduled cleanup removes expired unattached temporary files after 24 hours. Actual foreign-key subject links remain authoritative; media usages are a read-only union of those links.
